Quynh-Lan Le

Hi, I’m Quynh-Lan (Lê Quỳnh Lan in Vietnamese). 🍀 I’m a Master’s student in Computer Science at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), where I also earned my engineering degree in Information Technology and graduated with a “Very Good” classification.

🧭 My research leans toward algorithms and algorithm optimization and machine learning. I like to understand why a method works, where its time and memory go, and how to make it faster, leaner and more reliable.

I’m always happy to learn something new, so feel free to reach out if you’d like to talk about algorithms, optimization or machine learning! 🤗

Besides research, I love climbing mountains. ⛰️ A good climb feels like a hard problem: choose a route, keep a steady pace, change plans when the terrain surprises you, and enjoy the view at the end.
