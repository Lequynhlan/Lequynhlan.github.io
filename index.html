Lê Quỳnh Lan

Hi, I’m Lan. I’m a Master’s student in Computer Science at Hanoi University of Science and Technology (HUST), where I also earned my engineering degree in Information Technology and graduated with a “Very Good” classification.

My research leans toward algorithms and algorithm optimization and machine learning. I like to understand why a method works, where its time and memory go, and how to make it faster, leaner and more reliable.

I’m happiest when I’m learning something new, and my master’s studies keep giving me plenty to learn. If you’d like to talk about algorithms, optimization or machine learning, I’d love to hear from you.

Away from the desk, I climb mountains. A good climb feels like a hard problem: choose a route, keep a steady pace, change plans when the terrain surprises you, and enjoy the view at the end. The little ridge on this page is my way of putting the two together.
